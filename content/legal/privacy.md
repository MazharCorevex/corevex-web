# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Corevex Solutions collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## Who we are

This website is operated by Corevex Solutions ("Corevex", "we", "us"), a software company with its registered office at Sikander Plaza, Canal Bridge, Vehari Road, Model Town, Hasilpur, Pakistan. For any privacy question or request, email hello@corevex.org.

## What we collect

**When you contact us.** Your name, work email, company, the service or offer you are interested in, and the project details you choose to share.

**When you apply for a job.** Your name, email, phone number if given, CV, profile links and anything else you include in your application.

**When you subscribe to our newsletter.** Your email address.

**When you book a call.** Bookings are handled by Calendly. Calendly collects your name, email and booking details under its own privacy policy and shares them with us so we can hold the call.

**When you browse the site.** Our hosting provider records basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to keep the site secure and working. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site, such as pages visited and time spent. It does not tell us who you are.

We do not ask for sensitive information such as health, financial account or government ID details. Please do not send it through our forms.

## How we use it

- To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and discuss your project.
- To review job applications and contact candidates.
- To send our newsletter, if you subscribed.
- To keep the website secure and understand how it is used, so we can improve it.
- To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

## Legal basis (UK and EU visitors)

We rely on your consent for the newsletter and analytics cookies, on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract when you send an enquiry or apply for a job, and on our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website. You can withdraw consent at any time.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the website and our business, and only as far as they need it:

- Vercel: website hosting and file storage for CV uploads
- Resend: sending form notifications, replies and newsletters
- Calendly: call scheduling
- Google Analytics: website statistics, only if you accept analytics cookies

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.

## International transfers

Our team is based in Pakistan, and some of our providers store data in the United States and other countries. Where data protection law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as standard contractual clauses offered by our providers.

## How long we keep it

- Enquiries and related emails: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we start working together, in which case our contract and records rules apply.
- Job applications and CVs: up to 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
- Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
- Analytics data: up to 14 months.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy in a portable format. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter using the link in any email. To make a request, email hello@corevex.org. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California, you have the right to know what we collect and to ask us to delete it, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

## Cookies

The site uses only the cookies it needs to work. Analytics cookies are set only if you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time from the link in the footer.

## Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and reputable providers to protect your information. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will act quickly if a problem occurs.

## Children

This website is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or the law changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
