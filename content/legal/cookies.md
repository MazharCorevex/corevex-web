# Cookie Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser so it can remember information between pages and visits.

## How to change your choice

Necessary cookies are always on, because the site needs them to stay secure and to remember your cookie choice. Analytics cookies are set only if you accept them.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page. If you turn analytics off, we delete the Google Analytics cookies. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

We ask for your choice again after 6 months.
